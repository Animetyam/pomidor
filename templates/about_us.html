Pomodoro Technique

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. The technique is named after a kitchen timer shaped like a tomato (Pomodoro in Italian) that Cirillo used as a university student to help him stay focused and on track.

Key Principles:

  • Task Selection: Choose a task you want to work on.
  • Set the Timer: Set a timer for 25 minutes (one "Pomodoro").
  • Focus on Work: Work on the task without interruptions until the timer rings.
  • Short Break: Take a 5-minute break after each Pomodoro.
  • Long Break: After four Pomodoros, take a longer break of 15-30 minutes.

This technique helps increase productivity, reduce distractions, and improve work habits. You can use traditional kitchen timers or apps on your smartphone or computer to implement the Pomodoro Technique.

Learn more about how to apply this technique in your daily routine and enhance your productivity by visiting Wikipedia.
